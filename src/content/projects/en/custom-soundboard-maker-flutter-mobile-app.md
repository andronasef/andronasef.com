## Project Overview

Custom Soundboard Maker is a cross-platform mobile app I designed and built on my own as a side project with Flutter. It lets anyone create personal soundboards for memes, pranks, live streams and group chats — just tap, record, and remix your own sounds.

What started as a weekend experiment has grown into a product with **300+ pro users**, **600 daily users** and **11K monthly active users**.

## Project Objective

The goal was to build a soundboard app that feels instant and is genuinely simple to use, to:

- Deliver zero-lag playback so every tap feels immediate.
- Let users create their own sounds instead of being limited to a fixed library.
- Work fully offline, with no account or sign-up required.
- Offer a premium tier that rewards power users without blocking the core experience.

## Key Features

- **Record, trim & import audio** — Capture sounds with the mic or bring in existing files and trim them in-app.
- **Customizable buttons** — Colors, images and emoji, organized into folders and boards.
- **Playback modes** — Loop, spam (rapid-fire) and random/shuffle for every kind of use.
- **Trending sounds library** — Discover and download popular sounds in a tap.
- **Offline-first** — Everything works without a connection and without an account.
- **Pro upgrade** — In-app purchase that unlocks extra features for power users.

## Benefits

- **Fast & responsive** — Low-latency audio that beats most competing soundboard apps.
- **Truly personal** — Users build boards around their own sounds, jokes and communities.
- **Proven traction** — Thousands of monthly users and a healthy base of paying customers, built by a single developer.
- **Cross-platform foundation** — A single Flutter codebase that is ready to scale.

A showcase of building, shipping and growing a polished mobile product end to end.
